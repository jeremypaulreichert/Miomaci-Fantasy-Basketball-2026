# Errata to the October 20, 2026 records (append-only)

Written about 6 p.m. ET on October 20, after Codex's post-draft review (`reviews/CODEX_POSTDRAFT_REVIEW_2026-10-20.md`). Each correction refers to an earlier file that is left unchanged. Where an earlier statement and this file disagree, this file is correct.

1. **Segment count.** The shared draft ran in 14 executed segments: H, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V. Package I was prepared and sealed but never executed. F was a separate aborted attempt and is not part of the final picks. Earlier text saying "13 runs" counted the continuations, not the executed segments.
2. **Picks after the first tipoff.** `FINAL_PICK_AND_SCORING_WINDOW_2026-10-20.md` says "roughly picks 117-130". The exact range is picks **124-130**. Pick 123 settled at 18:58:54.816 UTC, before the 19:00 UTC tipoff. Failed attempts at pick 124 also crossed the tipoff.
3. **Mistral's solo finish.** It finished at **20:44:10.778 UTC (4:44:10 p.m. ET)**, not "about 4:41". This is still before the shared final pick.
4. **Seal timing addenda.** `COUNTED_ADDENDA_2026-10-20.md` wrongly lists seals R and S as made after the first tipoff:
   - R was sealed at 18:25 UTC and S at 18:46 UTC, both **before** the 19:00 UTC tipoff.
   - Seals T, U, V and the final-rosters seal were made after the tipoff.
   - The seal tool's fixed phrase "before the first counted tipoff" is wrong for T, U and V. It is accurate for the final-rosters seal, which was made before the first counted game (19:00 ET / 23:00 UTC).
5. **Post-response seals.** These seals were made after their run had started and after its first model response arrived:
   - Q: start 18:14:10, first result 18:17:00, seal 18:19.
   - S: start 18:43:29, first result 18:45:58, seal 18:46.

   Deterministic decision IDs do not restore precommitment for these two runs. Seals L, N, O, P and T share the same minute as their run's start, so the seal timestamp alone cannot show which came first.
6. **Solo B continuations.** The Z.ai B and Mistral B solo continuation packages were never publicly sealed before they started. The final-rosters seal binds only their finished outputs, so they are locally journal-bound continuations without demonstrated prior public sealing.
7. **Per-pick resource limits were reset across continuations.** Each continuation gave the failed pick a fresh opportunity clock, a fresh research and correction allowance, and an empty research history. Logical pick 117 took 8 model requests across P, Q and R. Pick 124 took 11 across S, T, U and V, which exceeds the nine-call bound of a single segment. `DATASET_DRAFT_2026-10-20B/pick_attempts.jsonl` gives every logical pick's episodes, requests and cost exposure. 122 of 130 picks settled in their first episode.
8. **Budget caps.** Each of the 14 shared packages carried its own $1,250 cap. These caps are per-package reservations, not an experiment-wide bound, and not spend.
9. **Exporter corrections.**
   - The first full export, `DATASET_DRAFT_2026-10-20`, already used the fixed stitching that accepts segments with no settled picks.
   - `DATASET_DRAFT_2026-10-20B` adds the per-pick attempt ledger and shows empty segments as "attempted pick N" instead of ranges like "97-96".
   - The solo exports in `DATASET_SOLO_2026-10-20` computed each solo window from the solo run alone, which is wrong. `DATASET_SOLO_2026-10-20B` uses the common window, the shared final pick at 1792529601015 ms.
   - `SCORING_WINDOW_MANIFEST_2026-10-20.json` binds that window to the shared and all solo exports and to the rule's hash.
   - Use the B exports for any release.
10. **Treatment description.** Settings changed during the run: pick windows, per-seat output caps, recovery feedback, and fresh attempts per continuation. The results therefore describe an adaptive, organizer-supervised deployment of these ten configured models, not a fixed-condition ranking of companies. The suggested methods language in Codex's review section "Scientific interpretation" is adopted.
