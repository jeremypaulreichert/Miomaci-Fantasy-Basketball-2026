14. **Shared-versus-solo preferences.** Earlier text said the comparison is "mostly a measure of draft position, not changed preferences". That causal claim is withdrawn. Supported descriptions:

   - same-manager solo/shared Jaccard is 0.04–0.13.

   Availability, accumulated roster needs, settings, timing and chance all differ between the arms. Separating them needs a matched-availability test.

19. **Rehearsal wording.** There was a full offline 130-pick rehearsal of the plumbing. What was missing was live qualification with real providers on middle and late boards.

20. **Streaming and retries are hypotheses, not rules.**
   - Streaming is a candidate fix for the ~15-minute HTTP 504s. These records don't show which timer caused them, or that streaming resets it.
   - "504 and 429 are safe to resend" is withdrawn. A timeout does not prove that nothing was generated or billed, and a new generation can change the answer. Version 2 must classify each status prospectively and keep every attempt's exposure.

21. **Fallback neutrality.** A baseline autopick scored as empty still removes that player from the other managers' boards, so it is not neutral. For an AI-only primary arm, prefer a frozen forfeit/empty slot. Alternatively, run a separately declared continuity arm that uses an AI-precommitted ranking. No fallback was used in this draft.
